Trimmer Math

The box minutes are measured at half throttle, the swath overlaps itself honest, and the spool always ends mid-fence-line.

The runtime truth

Volts times amp-hours is the honest watt-hour ledger; divided by the motor's watts, the 30-minute box claim becomes the minutes the pack actually owns - a brochure breeze, in reach on a dry day, or outruns the sticker.

The swath truth

The 15-inch swath only cuts new grass once; the 0.75 overlap norm (labeled) makes it an honest 11.3. Honest swath times your walking pace times the honest minutes is the square feet a charge really covers - and whether the far corner waits for the recharge.

The line truth

Every bump feeds inches and the spool holds feet. Bumps a session times inches a bump is the session's line bill; the spool divided by the bill is the sessions you actually own before the spool cap clicks empty.

What it never does

It never counts box minutes as lawn minutes, never lets the swath measure its own overlap, never forgets the spool has an end, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the last fence line at dusk keeps the honest ledger.

Trimmer Math is illustrative arithmetic with labeled norms, not a measurement of your yard, your trimmer or your spool.
